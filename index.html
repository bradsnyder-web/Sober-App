    Savings calculator

    Count standard drinks: 12 oz beer, 5 oz wine or 1.5 oz liquor. A pint, a big pour or a strong cocktail can count as 1.5 to 2. Picking a drink fills in typical US price and calories; change them to match your own habits.

    Where these estimates come from

    Calories per drink: CDC (beer ~150, wine ~120, liquor ~100) and MedlinePlus (light beer ~105, craft beer 170+). Standard drink sizes: NIAAA. Out-of-home prices: median US bar beer about $6.60 (Toast, 2026); wine and cocktail prices are typical ranges from industry guides ($7 to $15 and $8 to $14). Home prices are rough retail estimates. Calories are the drink only, so the total is a rough estimate, not a weight prediction.
